## Produce one coherent reading

Each selected paper gets a complete M with its own core responsibilities, not five workers sharing one batch of counts. Share original requirements/corrections and source acquisition, then develop controlled independent contexts. m1/m4/m2/m3/m5 are responsibilities; the Main integrates their returns. Reader order is whole design and finite world → foundations → reasoning chains → experiments → return to the entire original.

- Begin with a complete system event, mechanism or argument that gives every later part a purpose. Keep proof obligations visible while temporarily collapsing details. Bind conclusions to their real evidence nature.
- Prepare original notation, scopes, units and routine operations. Prefer the paper's objects; permit short local semantic blocks with the original expression visibly mapped back. Avoid a new global dictionary that makes the learner translate twice.
- Teach a chain through original formula → meaning → justified intermediate steps → next original formula. Preserve simple connecting steps and explain what the result unlocks. Complete valid omitted work; label additional assumptions and genuine gaps where they affect the claim.
- Read every figure/table's conditions and axes, not just the caption or favorable trend. Put relevant overhead and unchanged/unfavorable outcomes into the same comparison.
- Start the original coverage pass early. Send material findings back to the relevant chapter; finish with a meaningful route through the original and the roles of its citations/appendices.

Use the actual task's learner guidance. “I don't understand” calls for a different explanation of the missing connection, not automatic reduction to trivial examples. Ask only useful, optional feedback questions during teaching. A prepared guide or mail delivery is not a mastery diagnosis.

## Make the actual artifact work

Retrieve relevant recent successes/failures and inspect their artifacts when they change the current decision. Apply the lesson in the current source and final pages. A note saying “fixed” is not the fix.

Maintain Markdown with actual embedded images and correctly rendered inline/display math. Follow the available PDF workflow for desktop rendering, page screenshots, PDF assembly and final visual review. Preserve original figure numbers/axes/legends and distinguish user annotations from author material. Keep headings/introductions with the formula or figure they introduce; keep the equation and necessary explanation together. Use a phone-readable width/size and real content density; a small-width desktop preview is not an iPhone test. Do not replace content QA with page/hash/overflow counts.

## Persist and deliver without closing other papers

Use `python -B <exact-distribution>/tools/codex.py paper --help` for deterministic operations. The addon records sources, guide mappings, candidate artifacts, read-back mail facts and per-paper return; native Codex still reviews meaning and uses connected Gmail.

Respect expected revisions and stable operation IDs. A returned paper is independent of other papers and of the parent task. If an artifact/source changed, inspect the stale candidate before using an old receipt. After an uncertain send, reconcile Gmail before resending. Verify the resolved authorized recipient, SENT and all final PDF attachments; only then record the paper's completion/return. After every paper and common work are done, use the actual core RETURN for the final chat response and lifecycle closure.

Personal PDFs, feedback and mail evidence stay private. Link an existing Paper Knowledge pool through its own rules; do not fabricate questionnaire choices, explanations received, understanding or cross-paper transfer. Resume from the original source/version, current example and next step.

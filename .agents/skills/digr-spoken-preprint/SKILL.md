## Run actual characters

Each character gets separate native outward M and inner D contexts, initialized from that person's own persona/background and current `packet`. Do not fork full controller history, shared backstage files, other minds or a complete scripted future into them. Shared disk is not OS secrecy; respect controlled reads rather than claiming stronger isolation.

D authors fictional inner speech, not hidden model reasoning. Fear, pride, selfishness, warmth, misconception and distraction may coexist. M's real words can contradict D; M can privately ask, reject or reinterpret, and D must actually receive the new input before responding. Avoid a fixed D→M→D approval pipeline for every utterance. Silence or an unresolved private exchange is allowed.

Submit only the originating context's actual words/actions with source and executor attribution. Give the next person actual preceding public contributions and their own new perceptions. Allow refusal, wasted efforts, mistakes and changes of plan. Do not write the whole dialogue yourself and relabel its lines as native actors. Sequential host scheduling is not simultaneous residence or measured human latency.

When the observed situation changes, reconsider relevant world activity; do not call all earlier proposals fulfilled. Permit ordinary unopposed self-actions; confirm important consequences, blocking conditions or effects on others with sourced world decisions. Distinguish completed action, promise and attempt. Accepted events are the record; the small state table indexes reusable constraints, not every physical detail. A modest unspecified prop can be co-created explicitly. Choose a natural ending with consequences still intact; the original seed's future is discardable.

## Publish and check

Use `preprint publish` to create a new edition. `phone/PHONE.txt` is self-contained enactment/listening input; `LISTEN.md` is the public log. Creator material stays outside `phone/`. Only explicit public event/perception projections, public cast and scene, and public-use language notes enter the phone edition. Do not copy raw spec/personas/world/inner events into it.

The phone performs the preprint's roles in natural sequence for listening. It is a single host interpreting a source produced by independent native contexts. It must not require the listener to answer or stand in for somebody. Different voices and timing need verification on the real device; text probes are only text probes.

Read the actual output, compare representative dialogue to the original native returns, check private canaries are absent, and run `verify` against the current source. Source identity and clean exports do not prove dramatic quality, listening improvement or successful phone Voice playback.

Annotate language after the real dialogue exists. Bind exact surface spans, meanings, communicative functions and register to public source events; verify frequency claims with actual corpus evidence or say unverified. Do not infer mastery from fictional actor success or a finished book. Deliver one current entry, the phone package, optional creator edition, sources and real limits.

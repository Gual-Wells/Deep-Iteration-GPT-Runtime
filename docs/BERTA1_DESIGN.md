# DIGR 5.0.0-Berta1 Design

## Scope

Berta1 is a convergence branch based on 5.0.0-stable.1. It selectively ports useful alpha5-r3 mechanisms and the V supplement without treating either branch as automatically correct. MCP, embedded UI, PWA, remote bridge and backend services are out of scope. The only new presentation surface is local returned audit data.

## Why client behavior differed

Identical personalization and model labels do not imply identical enforcement. iOS, Web and Desktop may expose different tool runtimes, lifecycle persistence, background suspension, monotonic-clock continuity and final-output interception. A prompt-only copy of DIGR can describe a protocol but cannot prove that a host delayed final output, persisted state or honored time. A fast Desktop answer is therefore an attachment/capability failure, not evidence that lower minima were satisfied.

Berta1 keeps three modes explicit: ENFORCED when repository transport, persistent workspace, clock and final gate are real; ADVISORY when some facts are observable but canonical delivery cannot be interposed; PROMPT_ONLY when required work cannot be evidenced. Only ENFORCED may emit canonical DELIVERED proof.

## Resolution boundary

Canonical order is `N,T,R,B,S,D,V,L`. Explicit semantic labels are global anchors and may move. The parser then enumerates legal ordered mappings for remaining bare tokens. Zero candidates is INVALID; more than one is AMBIGUOUS and reports candidate slot sets. Duplicate definitions fail even when values agree. This avoids both extremes: rigid order rejecting clear intent, and permissive guessing weakening contract truth.

## V and Main sovereignty

V(o) is a minimum count of qualified persistent distant viewpoints. Each V has a private revisioned ledger, exclusive positive clock ownership, semantic-distance and nonredundancy evidence. There is no peer-read or V-to-V messaging API. A V returns a compact value event to Main; Main alone revises Strategy/Candidate and decides acceptance. Failed V work is timed and logged but not counted.

## D, time and completion

D retains its public D(s) surface. D+/D−/Dx are internal native action directions rather than new public parameters. All canonical D is exclusive and one interval has one owner. ClockJournal publishes to memory only after durable append. Its projection defines T, t, D and V; D/V never inflate T. Structured completion adds objective coverage, evidence integrity, adversarial resilience and residual risk, while Berta1 accepts stable.1 prose assessments during migration and discloses structured readiness.

## Logs and privacy

Delivery materializes `logs/TOTAL.ndjson` and independent N/T/R/B/S/D/V/L files. They record behavior receipts, counts, policy, evidence references and aggregate times, including unsuccessful D/V activity. They deliberately exclude hidden chain-of-thought. The log paths are bound into the delivered run summary and artifact index.

## Delivery invariant

Stable.1's strongest rule is retained: final bytes must equal the current content-addressed Candidate. Finalizing prepares and verifies payload, proof, summary, envelope and logs before the DELIVERED commit marker. Any unmet or interrupted path remains INCOMPLETE/non-success.

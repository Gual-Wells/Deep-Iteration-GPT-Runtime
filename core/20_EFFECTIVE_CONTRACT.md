# Effective Contract

The Effective Contract combines pinned identity, exact U0, structural resolution, native completion, explicit hard constraints and source disposition. It freezes commitments, never StrategyState.

Public order is N,T,R,B,S,D,V,L. V(o) is the minimum qualified, nonredundant, semantically distant persistent viewpoint count. Main remains sovereign; private VLedgers exchange no V-to-V state.

Adaptive default source disposition is REQUIRED. Explicit `source=auto` permits a grounded REQUIRED/WAIVED decision; `source=off` requires WAIVED and zero S values. D(0)/V(0) permit quality-driven work beyond zero minima. L applies to D isolation evidence.

Capability negotiation is not part of intellectual calibration. It produces `ExecutionMode` and `AttestationLevel`; it cannot lower/remap the contract or prevent MODEL_NATIVE execution. Claims that lack evidence become unknown/non-canonical.

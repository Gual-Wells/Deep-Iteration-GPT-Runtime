# V — Persistent Viewpoint Channels

V(o) freezes the minimum number of qualified viewpoint channels. A viewpoint is qualified only when it has a persistent private VLedger, at least one substantive event, a nonredundancy attestation, an explicit semantic-distance account, a compact result returned to Main, and positive owned V_EXCLUSIVE time.

V channels do not communicate with each other. They may challenge premises, change discipline or coordinate system, preserve a minority hypothesis, or test a distant value function. They are not generic parallel samples: redundant restatements and one-shot opinions do not count. Main receives value events and remains solely responsible for Strategy and Candidate selection.

Failed, discarded and unqualified V attempts remain in V time and V audit logs but do not satisfy V(o). `V(0)` is a zero minimum, not an off switch.

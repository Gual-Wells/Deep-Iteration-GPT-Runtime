# Local Audit Logs

Berta2 requires no MCP, embedded UI, PWA, remote bridge or backend service. Each execution returns or persists `TOTAL` plus independent N, T, R, B, S, D, V and L logs.

N/R/S/n/r receipts remain inspectable. T records T/t/D/V projections; B records timing policy and verification. D/V retain proposals, attempts, outcomes and status for successful and unsuccessful work, and aggregate all observed owned D_EXCLUSIVE/V_EXCLUSIVE duration even when an attempt does not satisfy its minimum. L records target, evidence-backed actual and mismatch policy.

Canonical host logs are deterministic NDJSON, content-addressed in the artifact index and covered by the terminal semantic/audit digest. MODEL_NATIVE returns equivalent compact user-facing categories, marks self-reported/unknown facts and never fabricates host hashes. Logs expose behavior and evidence, not private chain-of-thought.

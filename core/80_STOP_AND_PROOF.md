# Stop, Delivery and Proof

Mechanical minima open eligibility to stop; they never compel low-quality delivery. Completion assesses objective coverage, evidence integrity, adversarial resilience and residual risk, while revisioned blocking gaps remain fail-closed.

`digr.commit_delivery` binds the exact final bytes to the current Candidate and performs two-phase `FINALIZING -> DELIVERED | INCOMPLETE` commit. Interruption before the terminal transition is non-success. Only verified DELIVERED may render:

```text
DIGR（N目标/N实际，T目标/T真实（+D真实时间，+V真实时间），R目标/R实际，B，Sₛ（n目标/n实际，t目标/t实际，r目标/r实际，b），D（s目标）/D（s实际），V（o目标）/V（o实际），L（e目标）/L（e实际））
```

The proof document, exact payload, summary, envelope and audit logs must all remain content-addressed and recoverable. Failed gates close INCOMPLETE with concrete blockers and never receive a success proof.
